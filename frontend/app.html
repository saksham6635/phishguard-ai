    Threat Signatures

    Primary Vishing Archetypes Detected

    PhishGuard AI continuously monitors for these primary social engineering attack profiles.

    Bank Fraud & OTP Theft

    CRITICAL (75+)

    Caller claims your checking account or card has an unauthorized charge, then demands you read the two-factor authentication code sent via SMS.

    Trigger: “Read the 6-digit OTP code”

    Law Enforcement Coercion

    CRITICAL (85+)

    Impersonating police, tax officers, or court officials. Threatens immediate arrest unless you settle penalties via prepaid gift cards or crypto.

    Trigger: “Arrest warrant active in 30 mins”

    Remote Support Hijacking

    SUSPICIOUS (60+)

    Fraudulent tech support claiming your computer has security viruses. Pressures you to download AnyDesk or TeamViewer to compromise your device.

    Trigger: “Install remote desktop client”

    AI Voice Clone Emergency

    CRITICAL (90+)

    Synthesized audio mimicking family members claiming they were in a car accident or arrested, urging an immediate wire transfer to a stranger.

    Trigger: “Send money right now, don't tell anyone”
    Safety Checklist

    Instant Post-Call Action Protocol

    If PhishGuard flags your call as critical or suspicious, follow these four immediate defensive actions.

    1. Disconnect Immediately

    Do not argue, confirm credentials, or listen to explanations. Simply hang up the call.

    2. Call Verified Numbers

    Look up your bank or institution’s official phone number printed directly on the back of your card.

    3. Lock Compromised Accounts

    If you shared any passcode, immediately reset credentials and request a temporary card freeze.

    4. Export Incident Log

    Use PhishGuard’s “Download log” button above to preserve evidence for legal or fraud reporting.
